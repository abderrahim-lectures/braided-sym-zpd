# Statement audit: `cor:triangular` against its sources

Scope: the Lean declarations that formalise `cor:triangular` in `lean/ZpdCore/Triangular.lean`,
compared binder by binder with the paper and with the two results of Brešar's book that the paper
cites. Proof status and the axiom audit are reported separately at the end.

## Sources

**Paper**, `paper/braided-sym-zpd.tex`, `cor:triangular`:

> Let `A = Tri(A₁, M, A₂)` be as in Corollary 2.17(b), and let `f : A → A` be linear. Then `A` is
> determined by `μ(x,y) = f(xy)`. In particular `A` is determined by `xy + α(y)α(x)` for every
> linear `α : A → A` with `α(xy) = α(y)α(x)`.

Its proof in the paper: "By Corollary `cor:bresar-matrix`(b), `A` is zpd, and `A` has the unit with
diagonal entries the units of `A₁` and `A₂`. Apply Theorem `thm:zpd-fxy`, and then Corollary
`cor:anti` for the last claim."

**Brešar 2021**, Corollary 2.17(b), printed p. 25:

> The algebra `Tri(A₁, M, A₂)`, where `A₁` and `A₂` are unital algebras generated by idempotents
> and `M` is a unital `(A₁, A₂)`-bimodule.

The notation is his §2.1, printed p. 19: the matrices `[[r₁, m], [0, r₂]]` "forms a ring under the
usual matrix operations … We denote it by `Tri(R₁,M,R₂)`". Theorem 2.15, printed p. 30: "If a
unital algebra `A` is generated by idempotents, then `A` is zpd"; its proof is Lemma 2.2, p. 18.

## What the audit changed

The first formalisation used **`Subring.closure {e | e * e = e} = ⊤`** for "generated by
idempotents" — generation as a *ring*. That is one reading of the reference's phrase, and it is
what his proof of Theorem 2.15 uses, but it is the **stronger** of the two readings: a subring of a
`K`-algebra need not be `K`-closed, so `Algebra.adjoin K s = ⊤` does not imply
`Subring.closure s = ⊤`.

The theorems now assume the weaker, unambiguous hypothesis `Algebra.adjoin K {e | e*e = e} = ⊤`,
and the ring reading is recovered by

```
ZpdCore.adjoin_eq_top_of_subring_closure_eq_top :
  Subring.closure {e | e * e = e} = ⊤ → Algebra.adjoin K {e | e * e = e} = ⊤
```

so the formal statement covers the source under either reading. (The proof of Theorem 2.15 goes
through unchanged: the set `R₀ = {r | ∀ x z, φ (x*r) z = φ x (r*z)}` is closed under the `K`-action
as well, so it is a `K`-subalgebra containing every idempotent.)

## What remains, and why it is not a hidden assumption

* `[Module K M]`, `[IsScalarTower K A₁ M]`, `[IsScalarTower K A₂ᵐᵒᵖ M]`: `M` must be a
  `K`-vector space and the two actions must be `K`-linear — otherwise `Tri(A₁,M,A₂)` is not a
  `K`-algebra, and "`A` is determined by `μ`" (a `K`-linear notion) has no meaning. Brešar's
  Corollary 2.17 sits in §2.2, whose standing setting is "algebras over a field", so these are
  part of his hypothesis too, not additions.
* `[SMulCommClass A₁ A₂ᵐᵒᵖ M]` is literally the bimodule axiom of the source.
* Universe: `K`, `A₁`, `A₂`, `M` live in one universe, and `Determined` quantifies over test spaces
  in that universe. A Lean proposition cannot quantify over all universes; this is the documented
  technical restriction of `ZpdCore.Determined`, not a mathematical hypothesis.
* `Tri` is an abbreviation-level structure with `fst`, `snd`, `thd`; nothing else is assumed.

## Proof status and axiom audit

`cd lean && ./check.sh` builds the six modules; for the `cor:triangular` chain it prints

```
'ZpdCore.adjoin_eq_top_of_subring_closure_eq_top' depends on axioms: [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
'ZpdCore.zpd_of_idempotents_generate'            depends on axioms: [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
'ZpdCore.Tri.closure_idempotents'                depends on axioms: [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
'ZpdCore.Tri.zeroProductDetermined'              depends on axioms: [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
'ZpdCore.Tri.determined_comp_mul'                depends on axioms: [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
'ZpdCore.Tri.determined_twist'                   depends on axioms: [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
'ZpdCore.zmod2_adjoin_idempotents'               depends on axioms: [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
'ZpdCore.zmod2_tri_zpd'                          depends on axioms: [propext, Classical.choice, Quot.sound]
```

No `sorry`, no `native_decide`, no project axiom in the file (the only occurrence of the word
"sorry" is the docstring saying so). The proof follows the paper: `Tri.zeroProductDetermined` is
Brešar's Corollary 2.17(b), proved through Theorem 2.15 and the generation of `Tri` by idempotents
(`Tri.closure_idempotents`), and `Tri.determined_comp_mul` is Theorem `thm:zpd-fxy` applied with the
unit `(1,0,1)`, exactly as the paper's proof says. `zmod2_tri_zpd` shows the hypotheses are
satisfiable.
