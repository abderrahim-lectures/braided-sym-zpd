## Hypotheses assumed (nothing else)

For `Tri` (all are the standard typeclass form of "`A₁`, `A₂` are unital `K`-algebras and `M` is a
unital `(A₁, A₂)`-bimodule over `K`"):

* `[Ring A₁] [Algebra K A₁]`, `[Ring A₂] [Algebra K A₂]`, `[AddCommGroup M] [Module K M]`,
* `[Module A₁ M]` (left action), `[Module A₂ᵐᵒᵖ M]` (the right `A₂`-action, Mathlib's convention
  `SMul Rᵐᵒᵖ M`), `[SMulCommClass A₁ A₂ᵐᵒᵖ M]` (the two actions commute),
* `[IsScalarTower K A₁ M]`, `[IsScalarTower K A₂ᵐᵒᵖ M]` (`K`-linearity of the actions, needed for
  the `K`-algebra structure);
* `Subring.closure {e : A₁ | e * e = e} = ⊤` and `Subring.closure {e : A₂ | e * e = e} = ⊤`
  ("generated by idempotents").

The right `A₂`-action `m b` of the paper is written `(MulOpposite.op b) • m`; the isomorphism with
the matrix description is recorded by `Tri.fst_mul`, `Tri.snd_mul`, `Tri.thd_mul`, `Tri.one_def` and
`Tri.mul_def`, so the statements 5 and 6 are exactly the paper's claims for that algebra (no
weakened variant, no extra hypothesis beyond the bimodule structure).

## Proof (the paper's proof)

* **Lemma 2.2.** For `φ` vanishing on zero products let
  `R = {r : A | ∀ x z, φ (x*r) z = φ x (r*z)}`. It is a subring: `mul_mem'` is
  `φ (x*(r*s)) z = φ ((x*r)*s) z = φ (x*r) (s*z) = φ x (r*(s*z))`. Every idempotent `e` lies in
  `R`: since `x*e*(z - e*z) = 0` and `(x - x*e)*(e*z) = 0`, both `φ (x*e) z` and `φ x (e*z)` equal
  `φ (x*e) (e*z)`. Hence `R = ⊤` by the hypothesis, and `φ x y = φ (1*x) y = φ 1 (x*y)`, so
  `ψ = φ 1`.
* **(b),(c).** `Tri` is as above; `(a, 0, 0)` is in the closure for every `a ∈ A₁` and `(0, 0, b)`
  for every `b ∈ A₂` (`a ↦ (a,0,0)` and `b ↦ (0,0,b)` carry idempotents to idempotents and are
  multiplicative, so `Subring.closure_induction` applies), and
  `(0, m, 0) = (1, m, 0) - (1, 0, 0)` is a difference of two idempotents. Summing the three gives
  every element of `Tri`.
* **(d).** `zpd_of_idempotents_generate` at `Tri` gives `cor:bresar-matrix`(b);
  `determined_comp_of_right_id` with `e = 1` gives `cor:triangular` (5), and
  `anti_twisted_determined` gives (6).
