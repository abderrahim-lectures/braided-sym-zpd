# Statement audit: `rem:inner` and Brešar's Theorem 3.15 against `ZJpd.lean`

Scope: `lean/Research/BraidedSymZpd/ZJpd.lean` and `lean/Research/BraidedSymZpd/M2F3Bridge.lean`,
compared with the paper's `rem:inner` and with the two sources it cites. Proof status and the axiom
audit are separate, at the end.

**Outcome in one line.** The formalisation previously proved the theorem under the hypothesis that
the idempotents *span* `A` linearly; the paper's sentence, and the theorem it cites, need the weaker
hypothesis that they *generate* `A`. The two hypotheses are not equivalent — `M_n(K[eps])` is the
counterexample — so the old statement did **not** cover `rem:inner`. The file now proves the
generation form, following Brešar's proof, and keeps the span form as a corollary.

## 1. Source

**Paper**, `paper/braided-sym-zpd.tex`, `rem:inner`:

> In particular, for a unital algebra `B` and `n ≥ 2` the matrix algebra `M_n(B)` is generated by the
> idempotents `e_ii` and `e_ii + b e_ij` for `i ≠ j` with `b ∈ B` (indeed `b e_ij = (e_ii + b e_ij) −
> e_ii` and `b e_ii = (b e_ij) e_ji`), and a unital algebra generated by idempotents is zJpd in
> characteristic other than 2 [Brešar 2021, Theorem 3.15], originally [An–Li–He 2015, Theorem 2.1].
> So `M_n(B)` is determined by `μ_α` for every inner `α`, in characteristic other than 2.

**Brešar**, *Zero Product Determined Algebras*, Springer 2021, Theorem 3.15 (p. 49), verbatim from the
text used throughout this review:

> Theorem 3.15. Let `A` be an (associative) unital algebra over a field `F` of characteristic not 2.
> If `A` is generated by idempotents, then `A` is zJpd.

His proof sets `S = {s ∈ A : φ(x,s) = ½ φ(x∘s, 1) for all x}` (3.20), observes that `S` is a linear
subspace, proves `e ∈ S` for one idempotent `e` by the Peirce computation, and then proves
`e₁ ⋯ e_{n+1} ∈ S` by induction on `n` — using `t = e₂ ⋯ e_n`, the four Peirce pieces of `x` with
respect to `e₁` on the right and `e_{n+1}` on the left, and four Jordan-orthogonal products. Hence `S`
contains the products of idempotents, and since it is a subspace, it contains the subalgebra they
generate. Remark 3.16 records the conclusion as: `S` contains the subalgebra generated by all
idempotents.

**An–Li–He** is the same generation form. Their paper was not reachable, but the authors restate it
themselves — An–Li, *Characterizations of linear mappings through zero products or zero Jordan
products*, Electron. J. Linear Algebra 31 (2016) 408–424, p. 409 and Lemma 4.1 (pp. 419):

> We denote by `L(A)` the linear span of all idempotents in `A`, and by `J(A)` the subalgebra of `A`
> generated algebraically by all idempotents in `A`.
>
> Lemma 4.1. [3, Theorem 2.1] … φ(a, x) = ½ φ(ax, 1) + ½ φ(xa, 1) for every `a` in `A` and every
> `x` in `J(A)`. Thus, `A` is zero Jordan product determined if `A = J(A)`.

with `[3]` = An–Li–He. The same page attributes the *span* form, `A = L(A)`, to Ghahramani, *On
derivations and Jordan derivations through zero products*, Oper. Matrices 8 (2014), Theorem 3.3
(over `ℂ`, for `x` in the linear span `ℑ(A)` of the idempotents). This matters below: the span form
is Ghahramani's theorem, not An–Li–He's, and the two hypotheses must not be conflated.

## 2. The two hypotheses are not equivalent

`(S)` ⟹ `(G)` is immediate: the span of a set is contained in every subalgebra containing it. The
converse is false.

**Theorem.** Let `K` be a field, `n ≥ 2`, `R = K[ε]` with `ε² = 0`, and `A = M_n(R)`. Then `A` is
generated by its idempotents and is not spanned by them.

*Proof.* Write `a = X + εY` with `X, Y ∈ M_n(K)`. Then `a² = X² + ε(XY + YX)`, so `a` is idempotent
iff `X² = X` and `XY + YX = Y`. In the Peirce decomposition with respect to the idempotent `X`

    Y = XYX + (XY(1−X) + (1−X)YX) + (1−X)Y(1−X),   XY + YX = 2XYX + XY(1−X) + (1−X)YX,

so `XY + YX = Y` gives `XYX = (1−X)Y(1−X)`; composing with `X` on both sides turns this into
`XYX = 0`, hence `(1−X)Y(1−X) = 0`. So `Y` is off-diagonal, and then `tr Y = tr(XY(1−X)) +
tr((1−X)YX) = 0` because `XY(1−X)` and `(1−X)YX` have trace zero. Therefore the linear functional
`ν(X + εY) = tr Y` annihilates every idempotent, hence their span; but `ν(ε e₁₁) = 1 ≠ 0` in every
characteristic. So the idempotents span at most the hyperplane `ker ν`.

For `(G)`: `e_ii` and `e_ii + b e_ij` are idempotents for `i ≠ j` and any `b` (the two cross terms
vanish), `b e_ij = (e_ii + b e_ij) − e_ii`, and `b e_ii = (b e_ij) e_ji`; these are all the matrix
units of `M_n(B)`. ∎

(Earlier in the review the passage from generation to spanning was described as "unproved"; it is now
settled negatively. Note that `ε·1` is *not* a good witness in characteristic dividing `n` — its
`ν`-value is `n` — which is why the statement uses `ε e₁₁`.)

**Certificate.** `results/zjpd-scripts/span_counterexample.py` (exact `𝔽_p` arithmetic, output
recorded in `span_counterexample.out`): the case `n = 2, p = 3` enumerates all `3⁸` elements
(`110 = p³(p+1) + 2` idempotents, as the Peirce description predicts), and the other two cases
enumerate the idempotents through the description proved above, computing each kernel from scratch:

| case | idempotents | dim of their span | `ε e₁₁` in span | `ε·1` in span | dim generated |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `n = 2, p = 3` (enumeration) | 110 | **7** of 8 | no | no | 8 |
| `n = 2, p = 5` (Peirce) | 752 | **7** of 8 | no | no | 8 |
| `n = 3, p = 3` (Peirce) | 18956 | **17** of 18 | no | *yes* (`3 = 0` in `𝔽₃`) | 18 |

Every listed element is re-checked to be idempotent before the span is taken, and the "dim
generated" column is computed by closing the idempotents `e_ii`, `e_ii + b e_ij` (`b = k + k'ε`)
under multiplication, so it confirms `(G)` independently of §2's hand proof.

## 3. What the Lean statement was, and what it is now

Before this audit (commit `007c066`):

```
ZJpd.determined_twistMu_id :
  ∀ {K} [Field K] {A} [Ring A] [Algebra K A], 2 ≠ 0 →
    Submodule.span K {e : A | e * e = e} = ⊤ → Determined (twistMu LinearMap.id)
```

After:

```
ZJpd.determined_twistMu_of_generated :
  ∀ {K} [Field K] {A} [Ring A] [Algebra K A], 2 ≠ 0 →
    Algebra.adjoin K {e : A | e * e = e} = ⊤ → Determined (twistMu LinearMap.id)

ZJpd.determined_twistMu_id :            -- the span hypothesis, now a corollary
  ∀ {K} [Field K] {A} [Ring A] [Algebra K A], 2 ≠ 0 →
    Submodule.span K {e | e * e = e} = ⊤ → Determined (twistMu LinearMap.id)
```

| source | Lean before | Lean now | verdict |
|---|---|---|---|
| `A` unital `K`-algebra, `2 ≠ 0` | `[Ring A] [Algebra K A]`, `h2 : 2 ≠ 0` | same | match |
| "generated by idempotents" | `Submodule.span K {e \| e*e = e} = ⊤` | `Algebra.adjoin K {e \| e*e = e} = ⊤` | **fixed** — the old binder was the stronger span hypothesis |
| `A` zJpd | `Determined (twistMu LinearMap.id)` | same | match (`twistMu id x y = x*y + y*x`) |
| coefficient space | arbitrary `V : Type u` | same | match (one universe, documented in `ZpdCore`) |

The new proof follows Brešar's, with the three pieces that were missing:

* `ZJpd.balancedSet` — his set `S` of (3.20), as a `K`-submodule;
* `ZJpd.idem_mem_balancedSet` — `e ∈ S` for one idempotent (the old Peirce computation);
* `ZJpd.balancedSet_mul_idem` — the induction step: `t*e₂`, `(1−e₁)*t`, `e₁*t ∈ S` imply
  `e₁*t*e₂ ∈ S`. Note the hypothesis is *not* "`t ∈ S`": the products with the two `1−e` factors
  are what supplies the annihilating partners, and `t ∈ S` alone does not suffice;
* `ZJpd.prod_mem_balancedSet` — every product of idempotents lies in `S`, by induction on the length
  of the list (the `List` form is what gives the reshuffled products `t*e₂`, `(1−e₁)*t`, `e₁*t` at
  the shorter length);
* `ZJpd.idemProdSpan`, `ZJpd.idemProdSubalgebra`, `ZJpd.adjoin_subset_balancedSet` — the subalgebra
  generated by the idempotents is the span of their products and lies in `S`;
* `ZJpd.determined_twistMu_of_generated` — the theorem, exactly as in the source;
* `ZJpd.adjoin_eq_top_of_span_eq_top` and the new proof of `ZJpd.determined_twistMu_id` — the span
  form derived from the generation form.

## 4. Missing links that were added

* `ZJpd.twistMu_id_tmul_add_swap`: `μ_σ(x,y) = m(x⊗y + σ(x⊗y)) = x*y + y*x` for the swap `σ`, i.e.
  the paper's `μ_σ` is `twistMu id`. Previously `twistMu id` was only defined, never identified with
  the paper's syntactic form.
* `M2F3Bridge.conjByUnit_diagUnit`: the paper's `α` of `prop:matrix` **is** the inner automorphism of
  `rem:inner`, the conjugation by the unit `diag (1,−1)`; and `M2F3Bridge.mu_eq_twistMu_conjByUnit`:
  the bridge's `μ` is `twistMu (conjByUnit diagUnit)`.
* `M2F3Bridge.determined_of_peirce`: the Peirce proof (`ZJpd.determined_matrix2`) and the finrank
  proof (`M2F3Bridge.determined`) are now visibly the *same statement*, so `prop:matrix` has two
  independent proofs of one theorem rather than two theorems.

## 5. What the formalisation still does not do

* `rem:inner`'s generation claim for `M_n(B)`, `n ≥ 2`, `B` unital (`b e_ij` and `b e_ii` in the
  generated subalgebra) is not formalised — the argument is three lines by hand (§2 above, and
  `rem:inner` itself gives it). The Lean therefore does not instantiate the generation theorem at
  `M_n(B)`; it instantiates it at `M₂(K)` (via `span_idempotents_eq_top`), which is what
  `prop:matrix` needs.
* Consequently the formalisation covers `rem:inner` for `B = K` a field, all `n`, and not for
  arbitrary unital `B`. That is a gap in *coverage*, not in the statements: the hypothesis of the new
  theorem is the paper's.
* The paper's `rem:inner` sentence itself is unaffected: it cites the generation theorem, which is
  what the sources state, and its `M_n(B)` argument is correct (§2). The one bibliographic remark
  worth an author's decision: the *span* form is Ghahramani's theorem, so a reader who compares the
  Lean corollary `determined_twistMu_id` with the paper should be told so; the file's docstrings now
  say it.

## 6. Docstring corrections found by the audit

1. The header said the span version is "An–Li–He, Theorem 2.1" and called it "the key step of
   Brešar's Theorem 3.15". Both are now wrong in the corrected form: An–Li–He's theorem is the
   generation form (as they restate it, Lemma 4.1 of ELA 31 (2016)), the span form is Ghahramani's,
   and Brešar's proof of 3.15 is a separate induction, not an application of the span lemma. The
   header now says this.
2. `determined_twistMu_conjByUnit` was described as holding "for **every** unit `D`"; the declaration
   is inside `section Matrix2`, so `D` ranges over the units of `M₂(K)`. The docstring now names the
   restriction.
3. The header now records the counterexample of §2, so that the span version is not mistaken for the
   paper's hypothesis again.

## 7. What is not a hidden assumption

* `Ring A` supplies the unit; `Algebra K A` supplies centrality of the scalars (used in the
  submodule axioms of `balancedSet` and in the final `τ`, nothing else).
* `2 ≠ 0` is used once, at `inv_mul_cancel₀ h2`; in characteristic 2 both sides of the one-idempotent
  identity vanish identically under the zero-product hypothesis, so the method yields no
  information there. `2 ≠ 0` is the paper's standing hypothesis.
* No commutativity, no symmetry of `φ`, no finite dimension, no `Nontrivial A`, no associativity
  beyond that of `A`. `Algebra.adjoin` is the unital subalgebra generated by the set.
* `Determined` quantifies its coefficient space `V` in the universe of `A` — the documented technical
  restriction of `ZpdCore.Determined`, not a mathematical hypothesis.
