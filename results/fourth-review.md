# Fourth review: sources, statements, and the Lean development

Scope: `paper/braided-sym-zpd.tex` (v8 on this branch) and `lean/`. The three works the paper
cites were available as PDFs this time (`braided-sym-zpd-v7-references.zip`): Adrabi 2026,
Brešar 2021, Horn–Johnson 2013. Every locator and every recalled statement was compared with the
source text; every proof of the paper's own results was read again; the Lean development was
rebuilt so that it compiles from this repository alone.

Page numbers are the **printed** ones (they differ from the PDF page numbers by a non-constant
offset in the Brešar file).

## 2. Verified correct (no change)

Brešar 2021 — Definition 1.1 (p. 4: nonassociative algebras over a field); Proposition 1.3 (i)–(iv),
including (iv) "for every vector space `X`" (pp. 4–5); Definition 3.14 (p. 48); Corollary 2.17(a),(b)
(p. 25); Example 1.10 (pp. 8–9: every local derivation of a unital zpd associative algebra is a
derivation); Example 2.19 (p. 25); Example 2.23 (p. 26); Theorem 2.27 (p. 30); Definition 7.1 and the
convention that the algebras of §7 are associative (p. 113); Theorem 7.2 **including** its "Moreover,
… `c = T(1)` is invertible … `T(x) = cΦ(x) = Φ(x)c`" clause (pp. 114–115); §7.1 Banach–Lamperti
(p. 114); §7.3 opening (p. 126); Preface (pp. v–vi).

Adrabi 2026 — Definition 3.1 (p. 5: `σ` a `K`-linear **involution** of `A ⊗ A`, identity (1));
Remark 3.4 (p. 6: `BSDer_σ(A)` is the set of derivations of the nonassociative algebra `(A, ·_σ)`);
§1 (the symmetrized product `x ·_σ y = μ(x⊗y) + μ(σ(x⊗y))` with `σ² = id`); §4 (the twisted swap
`σ_α(x⊗y) = α(y)⊗α(x)`, product `xy + α(y)α(x)`, with `α` involutive of either kind). So both
statements of the paper about the preprint — "Section 4 twists the swap" and "we do not require
`σ² = id`, which differs from Definition 3.1" — are correct.

Horn–Johnson 2013 — §0.2.5, printed pp. 6–7, contains the reverse-order law `(AB)^T = B^T A^T`, so
"the transpose reverses products" is correctly located.

Brice–Huang 2015 — Theorem 2.3, checked against the authors' PDF
(<https://www.danielbrice.net/assets/publications/brice2015zero.pdf>): over a commutative ring with
identity, `(A, μ)` is zero product determined iff `T_μ = Ker μ`, where `T_μ` is the submodule
generated by the elementary tensors in `ker μ`. This is exactly `cor:criterion`, so the paper's
attribution ("stated for an arbitrary bilinear `μ`, over a commutative ring") is accurate.

An–Li–He 2015 — the paper is **not** among the supplied references, so its own Theorem 2.1 remains
unverified at the level of theorem numbering. Its content is corroborated twice: Brešar Theorem 3.15
(p. 49) states the same result and credits [24], which the bibliography (p. 176) identifies as
An, Li, He, LAA 475 (2015) 90–93; and An–Li's 2016 ELA paper quotes it as "[3, Theorem 2.1]".

Both theory files import Mathlib only, contain no `sorry` and no added axiom: the `#print axioms`
blocks report `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound` for every theorem, and the three
numerical theorems of `M2F3` depend on no axiom at all (`decide` is a kernel check, not
`native_decide`). `M2F3` needs `set_option maxHeartbeats 4000000` and `maxRecDepth 1000000`: with
the default limits the kernel cannot finish the `decide`, and the whole file still elaborates in
about 25 s.

The layout follows the TauCeti project (`github.com/TauCetiProject/TauCeti`, cloned and read for
this pass): `module` with `public import`, a single `@[expose] public section`, the copyright
header and the `# Title / ## Main declarations` docstring convention, `globs` in the lakefile with
`rev = "master"` for Mathlib and the exact pin left to `lake-manifest.json`, every line within 100
columns, no `sorry`, no `native_decide`, and an explicit axiom audit. TauCeti has no
zero-product-determined or bilinear-determination material, so nothing of its mathematics could
be reused — only the conventions.

What is **not** formalised, and why:

* the passage from the three numbers of `prop:matrix` to `Determined μ_α` — the dimension
  bookkeeping through `cor:criterion` — is not carried out in Lean. The numbers are kernel-checked,
  and the criterion is proved, but the bridge between them (a `finrank` computation for
  `Z_{μ_α}` and `ker μ̄_α` inside `M₂(𝔽₃) ⊗ M₂(𝔽₃)`) is not written. `prop:matrix` therefore
  remains checked by computation, as in the paper.
* `rem:inner` is formalised as the identity `twistMu (conj D) x y = (xD⁻¹)(Dy) + (Dy)(xD⁻¹)` and the
  equivalence `determined_twist_conj_iff` (determined by `μ_α` iff determined by the Jordan product,
  for every unit `D`), with the transport of `Z_μ` and of `ker μ̄` along `Φ`.
* `cor:triangular` is not formalised: it needs Brešar's Corollary 2.17(b), i.e. the zpd-ness of
  `Tri(A₁,M,A₂)`, which is not part of this development.

Two implementation notes, recorded so that they are not "fixed" later: `Determined` quantifies over
`V : Type u` with `K` and `A` in the same universe — a proposition in Lean cannot quantify over
every universe, so this is a technical restriction, documented in the file; and `twisted_determined`
assumes only that `α` is multiplicative, not that it is a unital algebra homomorphism, which is
strictly more general than `cor:twisted`.

## 4. Open points for the author

1. `thm:codim` is true but weak: its three hypotheses force `μ(x,y) = c(x)c(y) f(1)` (the theorem's
   own proof shows it), so `μ` is a scalar bilinear form times the fixed vector `f(1)`. The criterion
   of `cor:criterion` applies to every `μ` and decides the question exactly; restating the theorem is
   the author's decision (a form of it was explored in `results/deep-review-v8.md`).
2. `rem:inner` is mathematics added by a review pass, not by the author; it is correct as it now
   stands (and `q:noncomm` depends on it), but the author must either accept it or drop it.
3. The abstract's clause "and hence when the braided-symmetric derivations of that product can be
   studied through zero products" promises more than `rem:derivations` delivers. On the dual numbers
   with `α(a+bε) = a−bε`, the map `D(a+bε) = a` satisfies the hypothesis of `rem:derivations` (its
   defect vanishes on the `μ_α`-zero pairs) but is not a braided-symmetric derivation:
   `D(x ·_α y) = 2` against `D(x) ·_α y + x ·_α D(y) = 4` at `x = y = 1`, in characteristic
   other than `2`. Determination by `μ_α` therefore does not by itself let one recognise
   derivations; `rem:derivations` only says that the defect factors through the product. A repair
   was proposed in the earlier review and not applied; the author's decision.
