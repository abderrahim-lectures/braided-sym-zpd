# The core of the paper's Lean development

Two Mathlib-only modules. Both are built by `../check.sh`; neither has a `sorry` or an added axiom,
and the `#print axioms` blocks report only `propext`, `Classical.choice` and `Quot.sound`.

## `Zpd.lean` — determined by a bilinear map

The paper's general theory over an arbitrary field, for an arbitrary bilinear `μ`, with no
unitality, associativity or finiteness assumptions where the paper has none:

| Paper | Declaration |
|---|---|
| `cor:criterion` | `determined_iff` |
| `thm:zpd-fxy` | `determined_comp_of_right_id` (the unit used only as a right identity) |
| `prop:injective` | `determined_of_injective_comp` |
| `cor:identity` (ii), char-2 remark | `identity_determined_iff`, `identity_determined`, `determined_zero` |
| `cor:twisted` | `twisted_determined` (α only multiplicative) |
| `cor:anti` | `twistMu_eq_compMu_of_anti` + `anti_twisted_determined` (no commutativity) |
| `thm:codim` | `codim_determined`, `codim_determined_of_f_one_zero` |
| the two "not of the form `f(xy)`" assertions | `jordan_forces_comm`, `twisted_forces_comm` |
| the abstract's three-braidings sentence | `three_braidings_commutative` (α not assumed unital) |

## `Triangular.lean` — the triangular algebra and `cor:triangular`

* `zpd_of_idempotents_generate`: Brešar's Theorem 2.15 (p. 49), proved by his Lemma 2.2 (p. 18).
* `Tri A₁ M A₂`: the algebra of matrices `[[a, m], [0, b]]`, with its `Ring` and `Algebra K`
  instances and its unit `(1, 0, 1)`.
* `Tri.closure_idempotents`: Brešar's Corollary 2.5 — it is generated by idempotents when `A₁`
  and `A₂` are.
* `Tri.determined_comp_mul` and `Tri.determined_twist`: the two halves of `cor:triangular`.
* `zmod2_tri_zpd`: a non-vacuity check.

Provenance: written by review passes (Open Math OS), not by the author.
